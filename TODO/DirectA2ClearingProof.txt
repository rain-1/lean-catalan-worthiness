Direct proof of the audited A2 coefficient clearing
===================================================

For natural numbers 0 <= v <= u put

  F(u,v) = (2u)! v! / (u! (2v)! (u-v)!).

For every positive integer d,

  floor(u/d) + floor(2v/d) + floor((u-v)/d)
    <= floor(2u/d) + floor(v/d).                         (1)

Proof.  Write u=qd+a and v=rd+b with 0<=a,b<d.  If b<=a,
then r<=q, floor((u-v)/d)=q-r, and monotonicity gives
floor(2b/d)<=floor(2a/d).  If a<b, then r<q and
floor((u-v)/d)=q-r-1; both floor(2a/d) and floor(2b/d)
belong to {0,1}.  These two cases give (1).

Apply (1) with d=p^i and sum over i>=1.  Legendre's formula
then says that, for every prime p,

  v_p(u! (2v)! (u-v)!) <= v_p((2u)! v!).

Consequently F(u,v) is an integer.

For 0<=j<=3n, direct cancellation in the audited formula gives

  4^(7n-j) A2(n,j)
    = 2 choose(3n,j) F(4n+j,j) F(3n,j).                 (2)

Every factor on the right of (2) is integral, proving

  4^(7n-j) A2(n,j) in Z.

Conservative Pochhammer clearing
--------------------------------

For 0 <= nu < 3n, Legendre's formula also gives

  (2nu+1)! | D_(6n) 4^nu (nu!)^2.

At p=2, the last factor 2nu+1 is odd and
v_2((2nu)!) <= 2nu, which is supplied by 4^nu.  At an odd prime p,
put e=floor(log_p(2nu+1)).  At every p-adic level,

  floor((2nu+1)/p^i) <= 2 floor(nu/p^i)+1.

There are e relevant levels, and p^e <= 2nu+1 <= 6n, so D_(6n)
supplies the remaining e powers.  Therefore

  D_(6n) nu!/(3/2)_nu in Z.

This is formalized by `two_mul_add_one_div_le`,
`pochhammer_factorial_dvd`, and
`Dlcm_mul_factorial_div_pochhammer_isInt` in the same Lean file.

Simple-pole clearing
--------------------

The backward triangular equation propagates integrality from the diagonal
term A2(n,j) M(n,j).  After multiplication by 4^(7n-j)D_(6n), the kernel
h_l=choose(2l,l)/4^l cancels the l-step difference in powers of four.
The A1 term is supplied by descending induction, while the A2/l term is
cleared because l divides D_(6n).

It remains to clear the long odd denominators

  m = 2(j+r)+1,  0 <= r < 4n.

Write a=v_p(m).  If p^a <= 6n, the LCM supplies the whole prime power.  If
p^a>6n, then m<14n and m is odd.  Any complementary factor greater than one
would therefore be at least three and would force p^a<m/3<6n, a
contradiction.  Hence m=p^a.  The LCM supplies p^(a-1).  At the Legendre
level p^a, the first factorial quotient F(4n+j,j) has a strict floor carry:

  floor((8n+2j)/p^a) - floor((4n+j)/p^a) = 1,

while the other three floors vanish because p^a>6n.  Thus F supplies the
last p.  This proves

  m | D_(6n) 4^(7n-j) A2(n,j).

The resulting Lean chain in `NesterenkoCoefficients.lean` is:

  one_le_factorization_nestFactorialRatioNat_of_pow
  long_odd_dvd_Dlcm_mul_nestA2ScaledNat
  nestA2_long_odd_mem_ZSub
  nestA2_diagonal_mem_of_long_odd
  nestA1_scaled_isInt
  nestCConcrete_scaled_isInt

Therefore both halves of the specialized Nesterenko coefficient-clearing
theorem and the conservative `4^(7n)D_(6n)^2 C_n` integrality theorem are now
kernel checked without literature or prime-window hypotheses.
