Exact Nesterenko period bridge: self-contained proof plan
=========================================================

This is the proof to be formalized.  It specializes Theorem 2 of
Nesterenko's paper to

  a1 = a2 = a3 = 4n,   b1 = b2 = 7n.

No statement from that theorem is to remain as a Lean hypothesis.

1. The rational series term
---------------------------

Put

  R_n(t) = (3n)! (3/2)_(3n-1)/(4n)!
           * prod_(r<4n) (t-r)
           / prod_(r<=3n) (t+r+1/2)^2.

For a natural number v define

  w_v = v! (v+1/2)/(3/2)_v.

This is v!/(3/2)_(v-1), including v=0 with the natural continued
interpretation (3/2)_(-1)=2.  Direct cancellation gives

  J_n = sum_(v>=0) w_v R_n(v)
      = sum_(k>=0) w_(4n+k) R_n(4n+k),                 (P1)

because R_n(v)=0 for v<4n.  The second series has initial term P_n and
successive quotient

  ((4n+k+1/2)(4n+k+1)^2) /
  ((7n+k+3/2)^2(k+1)).

Consequently its kth term is exactly

  P_n (4n+1/2)_k (4n+1)_k^2 /
      ((7n+3/2)_k^2 k!),                               (P2)

which proves that the rational coefficient series underlying `nestJ2`
is the series in (P1).  The proof of (P2) should be by induction on k;
only the k=0 factorial identity and the displayed quotient are needed.

2. The finite partial-fraction identity
---------------------------------------

Let

  q_j(t) = (t+1)...(t+j)/(t+1/2)_(j+1).

The exact identity to prove in Q(t) is

  R_n(t) = sum_(j=0)^(3n) q_j(t)
             (A1(n,j) + A2(n,j)/(t+j+1/2)).             (P3)

For A2 use the positive audited factorial formula.  At the pole
t=-j-1/2, multiplying by (t+j+1/2)^2 and evaluating gives precisely
that formula.  After subtracting all double-pole terms, equality of the
simple-pole coefficients from the largest j downwards is exactly
`nestA1_triangular`.  Thus the difference of the two sides of (P3) has
no finite poles.  Both sides tend to zero at infinity (the left side has
degree -2(3n+1)+4n, and the coefficient of t^(-1) on the right is
`sum_j A1(n,j)`).  Hence the difference is the zero polynomial and, at
the same time,

  sum_(j=0)^(3n) A1(n,j) = 0.                           (P4)

This argument should be implemented with `RatFunc` numerator and
denominator lemmas, not by an imported uniqueness assumption.

3. Summing the basis
--------------------

For natural v,j, cancellation of rising factorials gives

  w_v q_j(v) = (v+j)!/(3/2)_(v+j),                      (P5)

and

  w_v q_j(v)/(v+j+1/2)
    = (v+j)!/((3/2)_(v+j)(v+j+1/2)).                   (P6)

After inserting (P3) in (P1), shift m=v+j in each finite outer sum.
The missing prefixes are exactly the finite double sum defining
`nestCConcrete`.  Therefore

  J_n = (sum_j A1(n,j)) f1(1)
        + (sum_j A2(n,j)) f2(1) - C_n.

Using (P4) and `nestB` this becomes

  J_n = nestB(n) * f2(1) - nestCConcrete(n).             (P7)

All interchanges here are a finite sum with a convergent series; no
Fubini theorem for two infinite sums is needed.

4. The 2-adic special value
---------------------------

Define in Q_2

  f2_2 = sum_(m>=0)
           m!/((3/2)_m(m+1/2)).                         (P8)

Its summability follows directly from the factorial formula and the
2-adic valuation tending to +infinity.  Prove the Nielsen/Beukers
special-value identity internally,

  f2_2 = xiCat/2 = 4 GZ2,                               (P9)

where the second equality is already `GZ2_eq_xi_div_eight`.
The first equality must be a proved hypergeometric transformation (or
an explicit telescoping identity between convergent partial sums); it
must not be introduced as a hypothesis.

Taking the Q_2 sum in (P7), applying (P9), and using `nestB` gives

  nestJ2 n = 4 * nestB n * GZ2 - nestCConcrete n.        (P10)

Together with `nestJ2_v2`, (P10) removes the last p-adic field from the
concrete `NesterenkoFormInputs` package.

5. Real form
------------

The same rational coefficient identity (P2) holds over R.  Absolute
convergence permits the real interpretation, and Euler's beta integral
(proved by expanding (1-xy)^(-4n-1), followed by two beta integrals)
identifies it with the positive double integral.  Nielsen's classical
real identity f2(1)=4*catalanReal then yields

  J_n(real) = 4 * nestB n * catalanReal - nestCConcrete n.

This supplies the exact equality needed to turn the raw J-rate into the
integer normalized linear-form rate.
