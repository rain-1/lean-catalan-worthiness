Nesterenko (4,7) row: proof plan and formalization contract
=============================================================

This note turns TODO/help.txt into a self-contained list of proofs to be
formalized.  It deliberately separates results which follow from the currently
checked project from the missing row-definition layer.

1. Explicit finite row
----------------------

For 0 <= j <= 3n define

 A2(n,j) = 2^(-14n+2j+1) *
   (8n+2j)! j! (6n)! /
   ((4n)! (4n+j)! (3n-j)!^2 (2j)!^2),

 B(n) = sum_{j=0}^{3n} A2(n,j).

The checked development now defines `nestA2`, `nestB`, and the original
partial-fraction `nestC` in `RequestProject/NesterenkoRow.lean`.  The numerator
is parameterized by the simple-pole sequence A1(n,j), so the specialized A1
coefficient-clearing theorem can be used directly.  The normalizations to prove
are

 aN(n) = 4^(7n+1) B(n),   yN(n) = 4^(7n) D_(6n)^2 C(n),

with integer-valued aN and yN.  The conservative integrality proof is termwise:
use the supplied coefficient clearing, D_(6n) * nu!/(3/2)_nu integral, and
2 D_(6n)/(2nu+1) integral.

2. Exact denominator valuation
-------------------------------

Legendre's formula v2(m!) = m - s2(m), applied to A2, yields

 v2(A2(n,j)) = -14n + 2j + 1 + s2(n) - s2(3n)
                + 2 s2(3n-j) + s2(j).                         (V1)

Here use s2(2m)=s2(m), s2(8n+2j)=s2(4n+j), and s2(6n)=s2(3n).
At j=0 this is

 v2(A2(n,0)) = 1 + s2(n) + s2(3n) - 14n.                      (V2)

For 1 <= j <= 3n, subtraction of (V2) from (V1), followed by
s2(a+b) <= s2(a)+s2(b) for 3n=(3n-j)+j, gives

 v2(A2(n,j))-v2(A2(n,0)) >= 2j-s2(j) >= j > 0.                (V3)

Thus A2(n,0) is the unique least-valuation term in B(n).  The
non-archimedean unique-minimum rule proves

 v2(B(n)) = 1+s2(n)+s2(3n)-14n,
 v2(4B(n)) = 3+s2(n)+s2(3n)-14n.                              (V4)

Lean milestones: prove binary digit-sum subadditivity, then the valuation of
the factorial quotient, positivity of each A2, strict-minimum valuation for a
Finset.sum, and finally (V4).

3. Linear-form valuation
-------------------------

The required period identity is

 J(n) = 4 B(n) GZ2 - C(n) = P(n) H(n),
 P(n) = 4^(7n+2) (8n)! (6n)! (7n+1)!^2 / (14n+2)!^2,

where H(n)=sum_{k>=0} h(n,k) and

 h(n,k) = (4n+1/2)_k (4n+1)_k^2 /
          ((7n+3/2)_k^2 k!).

The half-integral Pochhammer valuations give v2(h(n,k)) >= k.  Hence the tail
(k>=1) is in 2 Z_2, H(n) is a 2-adic unit, and v2(H(n))=0.
Legendre on P(n) gives

 v2(P(n)) = 14n+2-s2(n)-s2(3n),

so v2(J(n)) has the same value.  Combining this with (V4) proves the existing
slope-28 tail theorem.  Formalization requires a summability/tail-divisibility
lemma for the explicit series and a checked partial-fraction/period bridge.

4. Archimedean rates
--------------------

For J(n), use the positive double integral with

 Phi(x,y)=x^4(1-x)^3 y^4(1-y)^3/(1-xy)^4,
 W(x,y)=x^(-1/2)(1-y)^(-1/2)/(1-xy).

Writing u=x/(1-x), v=y/(1-y), and r^2=uv shows Phi <=
Psi(r)=r^8/((1+r)^6(1+2r)^4).  Its unique positive maximizer is
r=(5+sqrt(57))/4, and max Phi=(TminusNC)^2.  Positivity and continuity on a
small rectangle around the maximizer give matching upper/lower exponential
bounds, hence log J(n)/n -> 2 log TminusNC.

For B(n), Stirling applied uniformly to A2(n,j) gives the entropy function f
from help.txt, whose unique maximizer is (-15+3 sqrt(57))/4.  Since all A2 are
positive, max_j A2 <= B <= (3n+1) max_j A2, yielding log B(n)/n -> 2 log
TplusNC.  These are substantial new analytic developments: the project needs a
uniform Stirling/max-of-finite-sum library before they can be kernel checked.

5. Resulting dependency boundary
---------------------------------

After Sections 1--4 are formalized, the near-critical theorem can instantiate
the Nesterenko row directly and retain only the PNT input
LogRate (fun n => (Dlcm n : R)) 1.  The exact partial-fraction period relation
is the remaining named literature bridge for the intermediate build; replacing
the explicit definitions with an opaque structure would merely move, not
remove, the other assumptions.

6. Audited integrality material and its precise limit
------------------------------------------------------

The newly supplied full-LCM paper proves the following usable normalization
facts (with the positive A2 convention):

  * 4^(7n-j) A2(n,j) is integral;
  * 4^(7n-j) D_(6n) A1(n,j) is integral;
  * D_(6n) nu!/(3/2)_nu and 2D_(6n)/(2nu+1) are integral in the stated
    ranges;
  * therefore 4^(7n) B(n) and 4^(7n)D_(6n)^2 C(n) are integral.

The adjoint and prime-window material belongs to a sharper, separate
normalization and is deliberately not in this development's dependency graph.
The conservative `D_(6n)^2` clearing is termwise from the two specialized
coefficient-clearing facts and the elementary harmonic-denominator lemmas.

The second of those elementary lemmas is already formalized as
`two_mul_Dlcm_div_odd_isInt`: for nu < 3n, `2nu+1` lies in the defining range
of `D_(6n)`, so writing `D_(6n)=(2nu+1)c` makes
`2D_(6n)/(2nu+1)=2c` an integer.  The remaining Pochhammer-denominator lemma
is the next arithmetic target; it uses
`nu!/(3/2)_nu = 4^nu (nu!)^2/(2nu+1)!` and the supplied prime-by-prime bound.
